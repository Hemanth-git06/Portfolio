R1

Mixed Reality & MetaHuman Interaction

Developed a MetaHuman-based virtual instructor in Unreal Engine to guide users through real-world task sequences, including robot stacking workflows, within an immersive AR/VR environment. Supported research in human-robot interaction, robotics simulation, and human-centered computing by designing the agent's interaction flow, communication behavior, and in-environment presence to create a natural and responsive user experience. Built and deployed interactive 3D environments and assets on Meta Quest, integrating immersive visualization tools for realistic experimental setups.

R2

Real-Time Embedded Systems & Physical Prototyping

Researching the intersection of real-time embedded systems with modern interactive technologies, including Mixed Reality, game engines, and human-in-the-loop interfaces. Conducting research on latency-aware system behavior, with focus on timing guarantees, deadline handling, system responsiveness, reliability, and formal verification. Supporting physical prototyping through 3D printing by designing enclosures, mounting structures, and hardware components that bridge software systems with real-world implementation. Contributing to a drone build project involving embedded controllers, sensors, mechanical design, and real-time decision-making.

BIM, VR & Digital Construction
